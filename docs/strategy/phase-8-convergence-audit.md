# Phase 8 convergence audit

## Decisions

- Preserve the approved hero, Why Artifact and Wellbeing Studio art direction.
- Keep the homepage Paper-led. Ink remains limited to system, evidence and significant artifact moments.
- Keep Signal concentrated on consequence, state and the primary conversion action.
- Preserve the current navigation, CTA hierarchy, service architecture and evidence boundaries.
- Keep all representative and in-development work labelled at its genuine status.

## Bounded refinements

- Scope the Wellbeing Studio Ink surface to its selected homepage moment so the shared Work overview remains Paper-led.
- Present dense service inclusions as semantic, wrapping lists without changing the problem-led offer structure.
- Keep route metadata naming consistent with the visible Artifact masterbrand.
- Update repository status so visual completion is not confused with publication approval.

## Deliberately unchanged

The hero composition, Anchor, brand colours, typography roles, Why Artifact benchmark section, Wellbeing Studio specimen, Approach loop, Platform maturity language and public information architecture remain unchanged. No new Ink sections, Signal treatments, animation or speculative content are introduced.

## Publication gates

- A verified public contact destination is still required.
- ITC Avant Garde self-hosting and public-repository rights are still unconfirmed.
- `noindex, nofollow` must remain until launch approval.
- Final rendered keyboard, zoom, reflow and breakpoint acceptance requires an available browser session.
- Governed xAPI publication artifacts remain outside Phase 8 changes.

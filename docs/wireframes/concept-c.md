# Wireframe — Direction C: Editorial + Experimental Hybrid

Territory: Direction A's editorial backbone, with exactly one contained "systems moment" borrowed from Direction B's visual language — bounded, deliberate, not pervasive. The test for this direction is whether the systems moment earns its place or reads as a bolted-on gimmick; if the design review concludes it doesn't earn its place, that's a legitimate finding, not a failure of the concept.

## Navigation
Same restrained approach as Direction A (light, quiet text nav, Contact as a text link) — the hybrid's editorial identity should be legible from the header down.

## 1. Hero (editorial, one quiet motif)
Same asymmetric two-column layout as Direction A. The one addition: a small inline four-dot loop glyph (~24px, animating a slow single pulse around the loop) sits inline next to the eyebrow label, not as a section-dominating diagram — a signal that there's more going on beneath the editorial surface, without competing with the typography.

## 2. The problem
Identical editorial prose treatment to Direction A §2 — no systems styling here. The hybrid does not sprinkle the device everywhere; it appears once, deliberately.

## 3. The model — the contained systems module
This is the hybrid's one bounded "moment": a distinct panel, visually set apart from the surrounding editorial page (a bordered container, subtle background shift, like a magazine pull-out), containing Direction B's interactive four-node loop diagram at a smaller scale, with hover/focus disclosure. Everything outside the panel's edge returns to Direction A's plain editorial styling immediately above and below it.

Caption above the panel, in the page's normal editorial voice: *"Artifact treats the relationship between these four layers as a loop, not a hand-off. Explore it below."*

## 4. Selected work
Direction A's treatment (one full feature + text-led list) for the section as a whole — but the featured case study includes one small contained data panel (styled consistently with the §3 module, not Direction B's full readout language) showing a single illustrative event line, clearly labelled placeholder. One moment, not a pattern repeated across every project row.

## 5. Capability
Direction A's editorial column treatment, unchanged.

## 6. Approach
Direction A's horizontal text sequence, with one added moment of motion: a thin progress rule beneath the five steps fills left-to-right as the section scrolls into view (one-time, not scroll-linked/scrubbing), then stops. Reduced-motion users see the rule already filled.

## 7. Artifact technology
Same restraint and content as Direction A §7 — the hybrid does not over-expose the ecosystem; that fuller treatment belongs to Direction B.

## 8. Final CTA / Footer
Identical to Direction A.

## Mobile behaviour
Hero motif shrinks further but stays inline (never becomes a full-width graphic on mobile — it's a detail, not a hero image). The contained systems module in §3 stacks its four nodes vertically inside its panel, keeping the panel's bounded-container framing so it still reads as a distinct "moment" rather than dissolving into the page.

## Interaction notes
- Only two moments of motion on the whole page: the §3 module's interactive loop, and the §6 progress rule. Everything else matches Direction A's single subtle fade-in-on-first-view pattern.
- The bounded panel in §3 is the concept's core hypothesis under test: does a single well-placed systems moment communicate technical depth without diluting editorial authority, more effectively than A's total restraint or B's pervasive technical language?
